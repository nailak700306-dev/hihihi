What if your dog was dreaming about you while they sleep…?
You might have noticed it: sometimes your dog moves their paws, twitches their ears…
or even makes little sounds in their sleep.
That's no accident. Just like us, dogs go through REM sleep…
a phase when the brain is especially active, and when dreams are most common.
Researchers believe their dreams may replay moments from their everyday life:
running, playing, seeing the ones they love… and spending time with their favourite human.
So maybe, when your dog's paws twitch in their sleep…
they're simply reliving one of their best moments with you.
